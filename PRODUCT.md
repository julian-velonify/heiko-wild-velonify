## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS, plain multi-page (Startseite, Kollektion, Produkt), no build step. Chosen by the user.

## Users
Primary: professional users who buy instruments for daily work (Fußpflege/Pediküre, Kosmetik, Tierpflege/Veterinär, Dental, Praxis). Secondary: private buyers (e.g. Zeckenzange, Nagelknipser). German-speaking, in Germany. Job: find the right instrument fast, trust the quality, order without friction.

## Product Purpose
Online shop of Heiko Wild GmbH, Tuttlingen: stainless-steel instruments (Pinzetten, Scheren, Zangen, Feilen, Skalpell-Klingen & Halter, Dental) produced and sold by the manufacturer since 1989. The redesign must make clear on the homepage what the company is and sells, and make shopping simple.

## Positioning
Manufacturer from Tuttlingen (the German centre of surgical instrument making), handground, rustproof stainless steel, direct from the maker. A reseller could not truthfully claim "seit 1989, eigene Fertigung in Tuttlingen".

## Operating Context
Existing PrestaShop shop at heikowild.de. Categories: Maniküre & Pediküre, Veterinär & Tierpflege, Pinzetten, Scheren, Skalpell-Klingen & Halter, Dental & Zahntechnik. Prices shown incl. MwSt. plus shipping. Orders also by phone/email/WhatsApp. Hours Mo–Do 08:15–15:45, Fr 08:15–11:45.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the Heiko Wild logo (https://heikowild.de/img/heiko-wild-gmbh-logo-1614170190.jpg), brand green (#007b45) and the real product texts and images from heikowild.de. Must not look AI-generated; must read as a modernised medical-technology manufacturer, credible next to competitors.

## Evidence on Hand
Real product titles, descriptions, prices and images for ~35 products scraped from heikowild.de. Käuferschutz rating 4,87 shown on the live site. No real customer testimonials: do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. Say what it is in the first viewport: Edelstahl-Instrumente, direkt vom Hersteller aus Tuttlingen.
2. Find fast: category and filter first, clear names and specs.
3. Specs are the persuasion: dimensions, material, steriliseable, made-in facts over slogans.
4. Navigation and footer are plain and predictable.
5. Never invent claims, customers or terms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operable, readable for older professional users. Language: German.
